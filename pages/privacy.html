Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Welcome to Nexus Calculator. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it's used, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

Nexus Calculator is designed to function entirely locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, calculation history, or identifiable information to any external servers.

  • Local Storage: We may use your device's local storage solely to save your preferences (e.g., Light/Dark theme selection). This data never leaves your device.
  • Usage Data: We do not track your calculations, inputs, or usage habits.

2. Third-Party Services

Nexus Calculator does not integrate with third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics services that collect your personal data.

3. Security

Because all calculations happen on your device, your data is inherently secure. There is no risk of data breaches from our end because we do not hold your data.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact the developer.
