Privacy Policy

At Nexus Calculator, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how we manage your data.

1. Data Collection
Nexus Calculator is an offline-first application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your calculation data or personal information to any external servers.

2. Local Storage
Your calculation history and preferences are saved entirely locally on your device to enhance your user experience. You can clear this data at any time from the app's history panel.

3. Third-Party Services
We do not use any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising services within this application.

4. User Consent
By using Nexus Calculator, you consent to this Privacy Policy.
